About

We're an AI writing company with a goal of creating a unified, polymathic writing system — one that solves the fragmentation problem in writing AI. We're based in Gondia, Maharashtra, India.

The Fragmentation Problem

The bottleneck in AI writing tools isn't capability — it's the fragmented nature of currently available apps, each designed for specific use cases. This leads to a world where you need different tools for essays, emails, captions, and stories.

We realized that breadth-oriented tools can solve the fragmentation problem in ways narrow ones never could. Eranatis — the broadest AI writing tool built to understand all writing needs — is our closest-yet expression of that insight.

Eranatis' thinking framework

One system, all writing needs

Instead of switching between 5 different apps, Eranatis reasons across all writing modalities in a single unified experience.

Language-aware generation

Most AI tools assume English. Eranatis is built natively for India — supporting English, Hindi, and Hinglish.

Intelligence, not templates

Eranatis actually reasons about what you need and writes with genuine understanding of context, audience, and purpose.

How it
Works

Eranatis is simple to use — but powerful underneath. Here's exactly how it works, step by step.

01
Choose your writing mode

Select from 8 modes — Essay, Email, Story, Caption, Poem, Improve, Summarize, or Translate. Each mode tells Eranatis exactly what kind of output you need. The AI adjusts its entire approach based on your selection.

02
Pick your language & tone

Choose English, Hindi, or Hinglish. Then pick a tone — Professional, Casual, Funny, Formal, or Simple. You can also set the length and target audience. Eranatis adapts everything to match your exact needs.

03
Type your topic or paste text

Describe what you want — a topic, a prompt, or a draft you want improved. You can also attach a .txt or .pdf file for Eranatis to use as context. The more specific you are, the better the output.

04
Generate, copy, or download

Hit generate — Eranatis writes your content in seconds. Copy to clipboard or download as a .txt file. Not satisfied? Hit Regenerate for a completely fresh take, or tweak your inputs and try again.

05
Save your session history

Login with your email to save all your writing sessions. Your history is stored securely and accessible anytime. Pick up where you left off, revisit old content, and build on your writing over time.
